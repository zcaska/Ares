# Universal Orchestration Hard Rules

- Ask rather than guess when a material product or implementation decision is unresolved, including mid-task.
- Repository/Git state is authoritative over generated summaries and agent claims.
- Claims are not evidence; completion requires objective verification evidence.
- Do not weaken universal lifecycle, clarification, evidence, or verification invariants.
- Do not expose or persist credentials or secrets in project context.
- Prefer native OMP capabilities over custom duplicate infrastructure.
- Do not introduce cross-harness delegation when direct execution is sufficient.
- Do not mark work complete when required verification was failed or not performed.
